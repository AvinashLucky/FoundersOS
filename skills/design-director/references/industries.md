# Industry playbooks

What visitors in each industry need before they act. The generator already biases the
look per industry (`assets/genome.json` → `industries`); this file covers what the pages
must *prove*. Ids match `node design.mjs industries`.

## Contents
banking · fintech · trading · insurance · healthcare · diagnostics-lab · saas-b2b ·
devtools · ai-product · ecommerce · food-delivery · restaurant · mobility · education ·
real-estate · travel-hospitality · legal-professional · agency-studio · nonprofit-gov ·
logistics · media-publishing · portfolio

Each entry: **Trust** (what earns belief) · **Must have** · **Careful with**.

---

**banking** — Trust: regulator and licence details, deposit protection facts, security
practices, clear fees, real support channels. Must have: product pages with rates and
charges, eligibility, branch/ATM or contact, grievance redressal. Careful with: rates and
promises must be current and sourced; never imply a regulator endorses you.

**fintech** — Trust: who holds the money (partner bank/licence), fees in plain numbers,
security, how to get money out. Must have: pricing, how it works, compliance page. Careful
with: "free" and "instant" claims must be literally true.

**trading** — Trust: registration numbers, risk disclosures, transparent brokerage and
charges, platform uptime. Must have: charges calculator, account opening steps, risk
warning near every call to action. Careful with: no return promises; past performance is
not a guarantee — say so where performance appears.

**insurance** — Trust: claim process and real settlement figures with sources, regulator
details. Must have: plan comparison, what's covered and excluded, claims page. Careful with:
exclusions must be as visible as benefits.

**healthcare** — Trust: named clinicians with qualifications, accreditation, clear
emergency guidance. Must have: find a doctor/department, booking, visiting info, patient
rights. Careful with: no medical claims or outcomes without evidence; emergency numbers
prominent; accessibility is critical (older and unwell users).

**diagnostics-lab** — Trust: accreditation, sample-handling process, turnaround times you
can meet, report security. Must have: test catalogue with preparation instructions and
prices, home collection, report access. Careful with: never interpret results on the page;
patient data stays out of URLs and analytics.

**saas-b2b** — Trust: the product itself shown working, security/compliance page, real
customers (with permission). Must have: product tour, pricing, docs or help, security.
Careful with: integration and "AI" claims must match what ships.

**devtools** — Trust: docs quality, code examples that run, changelog, status page,
open-source licence clarity. Must have: quick start, API reference, pricing limits.
Careful with: benchmarks only with method and reproducible setup.

**ai-product** — Trust: a live example the visitor can try, what data is used and kept,
limits stated plainly. Must have: demo, privacy/data page, pricing. Careful with: no
"AGI", no guaranteed accuracy, label generated content as generated.

**ecommerce** — Trust: delivery times and costs before checkout, returns policy, real
reviews, secure payment. Must have: catalogue with filters, product detail, cart,
checkout, order tracking. Careful with: stock and delivery estimates must be real.

**food-delivery** — Trust: delivery area and times, hygiene/licensing, real menus with
prices. Must have: location check, menu, cart, order status. Careful with: food photos
should be the real dish.

**restaurant** — Trust: real photos, menu with prices, address, hours, reservation.
Must have: menu, booking, location, contact. Careful with: hours and closures up to date.

**mobility** — Trust: fare rules shown before booking, driver/vehicle verification,
safety features, support. Must have: booking, fare estimate, coverage area, safety page.
Careful with: ETAs are estimates — label them.

**education** — Trust: syllabus, instructors with real credentials, outcomes with
sources, refund policy. Must have: course pages, schedule, fees, enrolment. Careful with:
no placement or salary guarantees without audited data.

**real-estate** — Trust: registration numbers (e.g. RERA in India), real photos and
floor plans, clear pricing. Must have: listings with filters, property detail, site visit
booking. Careful with: carpet vs. built-up area stated clearly.

**travel-hospitality** — Trust: real photos, all-in prices, cancellation terms, location.
Must have: rooms/packages, availability, booking, policies. Careful with: no hidden fees.

**legal-professional** — Trust: qualified people with registrations, practice areas,
how engagements work. Must have: services, team, contact/consultation. Careful with:
professional advertising rules in many jurisdictions restrict testimonials and claims.

**agency-studio** — Trust: real work with credited clients (with permission), process,
who you'll work with. Must have: work, services, about, contact. Careful with: only show
work you're allowed to show.

**nonprofit-gov** — Trust: registration, how funds are used, reports, accessibility.
Must have: mission, programmes, donate/apply, reports, contact. Careful with: plain
language, multilingual where the audience needs it, WCAG AA as a minimum.

**logistics** — Trust: coverage, transit times, tracking, insurance. Must have:
quote, track, services, contact. Careful with: transit times as ranges.

**media-publishing** — Trust: bylines, dates, corrections policy, sources. Must have:
article pages, sections, search, subscribe. Careful with: separate sponsored content clearly.

**portfolio** — Trust: the work itself, your role on each project, contact. Must have:
selected work, about, contact. Careful with: credit collaborators; get permission for
client work.
