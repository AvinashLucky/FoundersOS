07

Pricing that works

Single asset

$9–$29 — one template or prompt pack. The entry buy.

Bundle / Vault

$27–$79 — 5+ related assets. The profit center (near-100% margin).

Productized service

$150–$1,000+ — "Your SaaS landing in 3 days." Premium, repeatable.

Rule of thumb: lead with a cheap single asset to win the customer, then sell the Vault. The bundle is where the money is — the perceived value is huge and your cost to add one more file is zero.
