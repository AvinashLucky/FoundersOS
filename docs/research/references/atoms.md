# Atoms (formerly MGX) — reference for FounderOS

**What it is.** The commercial product of the team behind MetaGPT, the open-source "AI
software company" framework. It launched as MGX ("MetaGPT X") and, per a Chinese tech blog,
was renamed **Atoms** in January 2026. Its agents cover market research, planning,
engineering and marketing, and it produces deployable full-stack apps with auth, database,
Stripe payments and hosting.

**Why it matters to us.** It is the closest product to FounderOS's idea: a team of
role-based agents instead of one assistant.

**Pricing (unverified; sources disagree).** One directory lists free / Pro $16 / Max $79 a
month. Another lists "from $20", usage-based. Older MGX listings show $70–500 tiers. Check
atoms.dev before quoting any of these.

**What to learn**
- Roles are shown as named teammates. Founders understand "the PM said / the engineer
  did" faster than "the pipeline ran".
- The output is a *deployed app*, not a document. People value the running thing most.
- It has an open-source core (MetaGPT), so people can trust and inspect it.

**Where FounderOS is different, and why that matters**
- **Disagreement is shown, not smoothed over.** FounderOS's debate round and the CEO
  resolving each challenge are the product. A consensus-only run is a bug for us.
- **Receipts:** every run shows model, cost, memory used and who dissented.
- **No lock-in:** every built project runs from a bare copy (the eject check).
- **Free-first:** an offline engine plus free model tiers, and you bring your own key.

**Honest gap.** Atoms builds and hosts apps today. FounderOS produces the debated blueprint
and the project folder, but its build runner (Track A Stage 2) isn't built yet. Closing
that gap is the top FounderOS priority, and RaKeGa Works is the first real test of it.
