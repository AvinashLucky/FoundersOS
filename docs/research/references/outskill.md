# Outskill — reference for RaKeGa

**Which product this is.** You said "Skill it". A web search found no AI-learning platform
by that exact name; the closest well-known match for this business model is **Outskill**.
If you meant a different one, send the link and I'll swap this study.

**What it is.** An AI upskilling company whose entry point is a **free 2-day live "AI
Mastermind"** (about 16 hours: prompting, workflow automation, building agents). From there
learners move into paid programmes such as an "AI Generalist" or "AI Engineering"
accelerator.

**Pricing (third-party, unverified, inconsistent).**
- Coupon sites list programmes "from ₹4,998", and one lists a figure around ₹1.14 lakh
  before discount.
- A competitor's review (not neutral) puts the cohort upsell at $1,000–3,000.
- Check outskill.com for real figures.

**What to learn**
- **A free, substantial live event** (two days, not two hours) builds real trust and a big
  audience.
- **Cohorts** (a group learning together over weeks) beat solo videos on completion. RaKeGa
  should run its live batches as cohorts and keep the recordings as the course.
- **Building agents and automation** is the topic people pay most for. It's RaKeGa
  course 3.

**What some reviewers dislike (Trustpilot; opinions, not verified):**
- High-pressure upsells, including a 20-minute "one-time reduced rate" window.
- Further upsells inside the paid programme.

Other reviews on the same page are positive.

**What RaKeGa does differently**
- **No fake deadlines.** A price is the price; no countdowns that reset. This is a rule in
  RaKeGa's CLAUDE.md §8.
- **A clear ladder:** free lesson → course → live cohort → "we'll build it for you"
  (RaKeGa Works). Every step is optional and honestly priced.
