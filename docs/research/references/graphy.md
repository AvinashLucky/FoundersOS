# Graphy — reference for RaKeGa

**What it is.** A course and community platform owned by Unacademy (formerly Spayee) that
many Indian creators use to sell courses. It offers courses, communities, branded mobile
apps, live classes and webinars, DRM-protected video and AI support from one dashboard.
**It is the closest product to RaKeGa Live + Learn**, but as a tool a creator rents rather
than a brand of its own.

**Pricing (third-party, 2026, conflicting; check graphy.com).**
- Launch: about $37–49/month.
- Rise: about $112–149/month.
- Scale: about $399/month.
- Sources disagree on transaction fees (0% vs 5–10%).
- One source says Launch allows webinars of up to 500 participants and 10,000 live-class
  minutes a month.
- Branded mobile apps are reportedly only on Rise and above.

**What to learn**
- **500-seat webinars are a plan feature here.** That's the bar teachers in India expect,
  and it confirms RaKeGa Live's 200–500 target.
- **Live minutes as a monthly allowance** is a simple way to explain live costs. RaKeGa
  should show the teacher "minutes used this month" from real data.
- **DRM and protected video** matter to creators selling paid content. RaKeGa starts with
  signed URLs and adds DRM only if piracy shows up.

**What some reviewers dislike (reported by a review site quoting Trustpilot; opinions,
not verified):** responsive pre-sale outreach followed by slow post-sale support.

**What RaKeGa does differently**
- RaKeGa isn't a rented tool: it is **our** academy plus our agency.
- **Live → recording → lesson is automatic**, with no upload step.
- **The learner's next step can be "have it built for you"** (RaKeGa Works), which a
  course-hosting platform doesn't offer.
