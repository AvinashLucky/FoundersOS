# Be10x — reference for RaKeGa

**What it is.** An Indian company founded in 2023 by IIT Kharagpur alumni. It teaches
working professionals to use AI tools through very low-price live workshops (press coverage
headlines "Rs 9 workshops") that lead into paid courses. The company reports 5 million+
learners across 800+ live workshops, and in 2025 launched a 4-hour no-code AI workshop in
the US. Topics: prompting, research, content, presentations, workflow automation.

**The model:** cheap live workshop (about 3 hours) → paid "mastery" courses → certificates,
tools and career programmes.

**What to learn**
- **A very low entry price** removes the "is it worth it?" question. The first class is
  the marketing.
- **Practical over theory:** they promise "use it at work this week". RaKeGa's levels
  (Starter / Working / Owner) should keep that promise.
- **Scale comes from live workshops.** Hundreds of people in one session is normal in this
  market, which confirms that RaKeGa Live needs the 200–500 capacity.

**What some reviewers dislike (Trustpilot; opinions, not verified):**
- A long sales pitch inside the workshop; one reviewer reports 90+ minutes about the paid
  course.
- A pace too fast to take notes.

The overall Trustpilot score is 4.5/5 from about 11k reviews.

**What RaKeGa does differently**
- **The free/cheap class teaches fully.** The paid offer gets at most 10 minutes at the
  end. That is a rule in RaKeGa's CLAUDE.md §8.
- **Every live class becomes a recording with chapters**, so nobody has to scribble notes.
- **An AI tutor per course** answers questions after class, citing the lesson.
