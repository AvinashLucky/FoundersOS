# Business Vision

> Canonical full text: \`/FounderOS-Product-Bible.md\`. This is the working summary.

FounderOS is a **Virtual AI Software Company** — one founder directs a coordinated org of AI
agents that discover, design, build, review, secure, and remember software, returning one
executive answer. Mission: **give every founder the leverage of an elite software company.**

Three forces: a coordinated agent org · memory that compounds · cost as a first-class citizen.
North Star: **Founder Leverage Ratio** — ship-ready multidisciplinary output per unit of attention.
