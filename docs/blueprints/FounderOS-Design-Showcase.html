02 · Design Principles

Eight laws of the calm command surface.

Distilled — not copied — from the deepest patterns in Linear, Raycast, Arc, Stripe, Figma, Notion, Cursor, Claude and Vision Pro: hierarchy, focus, rhythm, progressive disclosure, spatial depth, motion-as-meaning, and cognitive-load reduction.

P1

Calm by default, alive on intent

Stillness until you act; then cinematic, purposeful response. The interface breathes, it never fidgets.

P2

One focal plane

Exactly one subject is in focus; everything else recedes in depth, blur, and contrast. Focus is a managed resource.

P3

Depth over density

Spatial layering — glass, elevation, parallax — organizes meaning instead of cramming more boxes onto a grid.

P4

Motion is meaning

Animation shows the company working — agents converging, memory indexing, the graph linking — never decoration.

P5

Progressive disclosure

The simplest possible surface first; depth unfolds on demand. The org is invisible until you ask to see it.

P6

Three voices, one system

The company speaks in serif, the system labels in mono, your content lives in sans. Typography itself tells the story.

P7

Adaptive identity, constant substrate

Each project carries its own accent personality on one timeless dark substrate — variety without chaos.

P8

Reversible & legible

Every action can be undone and inspected. Reasoning, sources, and cost are one gesture away — confidence by design.

07 · Navigation & Page Hierarchy

You don't navigate. Context arrives.

A command-first spatial model. One persistent surface; depth opens as panels and shared-layout transitions, not page reloads. Three zones, no deep menus.

Command zone — always

  • ⌘K Intent line — the universal entry to everything
  • ↹ Project switcher — re-tunes the whole accent
  • ◷ Run timeline — live + history, scrubbable

Focus zone — the work

  • 1 Command Surface — the run & the answer
  • 2 Project Canvas — lifecycle stages as one continuum
  • 3 Memory & Graph — recall, reuse, connect

Depth zone — on demand

  • ↘ Show the company — agents, gates, debate
  • ↘ Cost & routing — spend, models, budgets
  • ↘ Founder Intelligence — brief, learning, decisions

09 · Accessibility & Honest Critique

Premium means everyone, and naming the flaws.

Motion is optional

Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion; meaning is never carried by motion alone.

Contrast on the substrate

Text meets WCAG AA on the dark base; accents are validated per theme, never used as the only signal.

Keyboard-complete

⌘K-first means the whole product is operable without a mouse; visible focus rings on the accent.

Calm for cognition

One focal plane and progressive disclosure reduce load for ADHD, anxiety, and first-time users alike.

Screen-reader narration

The "company at work" has a live-region text track: "Architecture passed review" — not just animation.

Risk: dark-only elegance

A pure dark substrate can fail bright-environment and low-vision users. Fix: ship a "Daylight" substrate variant that keeps Strata's depth model in light.

Risk: invisible-company anxiety

Hiding the org can feel like loss of control for power users. Fix: a persistent, unobtrusive "company pulse" and instant "show your work."

Risk: serif-as-voice legibility

Serif at small sizes or dense data can tire eyes. Fix: serif reserved for the company's voice and titles only; never for data or long forms.

Risk: theme accent drift

Seven accents risk inconsistency. Fix: accents are tokenized and contrast-locked; substrate/space/type never change.

Risk: cinematic vs. performance

Rich motion can stutter on low-end devices. Fix: GPU-only transforms, motion budget, and graceful degradation to fades.

Trendy ages. Calm, hierarchy, and meaning don't. Strata is built to still feel right in ten years.
